Your operating guide

1. Register and welcome a player
  1. The player creates an account and verifies their email.
  2. They read the receiving instructions, pay ₦5,000 and report the transfer.
  3. Check the actual credit in your banking app before approving the report. A report or screenshot alone does not confirm payment.
  4. The player completes their application. Review it separately and assign the correct squad after admission.
  5. The approved player can access their Acceptance Letter and private account features.
Review reported transfers
2. Welcome a coach without charging player fees

Share the coach sign-up link. Coaches verify their email and submit their own profile. Review the application and assign authorized squads. Coach-only accounts do not pay player registration or dues. An approved coach who also joins as a player retains their player obligations.

Coach sign-up page · Coach applications
3. Collect dues and reconcile refunds

Monthly dues are ₦3,000. Players can report 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months with a chosen start month. Confirm the exact amount and banking reference before approval; the portal then records covered months and a receipt.

For declined paid registration, complete the refund outside this website, then record its reference in the refund queue. Recording a refund does not send money.

Covered dues months · Refund queue
4. Publish training and protect private schedules

Create the session with its date, time, venue and squad. Publish an announcement when details change. For a public calendar event, select Published and Public and leave squad and private recipient empty. Keep individual appointments, attendance and feedback private.

Portal announcements appear in members’ accounts. They are not automatically emailed, sent on WhatsApp or copied into the separate public website news page. Share confirmed arrangements in your training group when needed.

Add training · Add announcement · Check public schedule
5. Resolve login and payment questions

Login verification uses email only. Check inbox and spam, respect the resend cooldown and use the latest code. Never request a player’s password or verification code.

If a transfer is awaiting review, ask the player not to pay again. Check the bank and the existing report. If details are incorrect, decline with a clear explanation before they submit a corrected report.
