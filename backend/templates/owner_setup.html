Brevo SMS: where to check

  1. Sign in at app.brevo.com.
  2. At the top right, open Usage and plan. Capture the SMS credit balance.
  3. Open your account menu → Settings → Campaigns → SMS Sender ID.
  4. Capture the Nigeria sender row and its status. If no sender exists, select Request Sender ID, choose Nigeria and use your accurate business details.

Sender requested by the portal: Warkfin. Select transactional messages for login codes. A sample is “Your Warkfin Academy verification code is 123456. Do not share this code.”

Do not include API keys, passwords or bank details in screenshots. On Windows, press Win + Shift + S, select the relevant area, then paste into the chat.

Credit purchases: account menu → My plan → SMS prepaid credits. Review the Nigeria price before purchasing. Email verification remains available while SMS approval is pending.
