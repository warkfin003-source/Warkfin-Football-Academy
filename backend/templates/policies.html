Privacy & participation

Warkfin Football Academy uses registration details to assess applications, organise training, contact members and identify emergency contacts. This initial portal is for adults aged 18 and above.

Your information

Personal application details are available to the owner. Assigned coaches can access football records, availability, attendance and feedback for their squads. Players can view their own private records and feedback shared with them. These records are not public rankings.

Contact Warkfin Emmanuel through the academy’s published contact details to request a correction, discuss removal of your information or withdraw photo/video permission. Information is retained only as needed for academy administration and applicable obligations.

Payments and appointments

Payment references, amounts and the months covered are stored for accounting and private receipts. Card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by this portal. Appointment requests are reviewed by the owner. Confirmed appointments and notifications are shown only to their intended members and authorised staff.

Participation

Attend punctually with suitable football kit and drinking water. Respect teammates, coaches, opponents and facilities. Abuse, harassment and discrimination are unacceptable. Tell the coordinator privately when you cannot attend. Report injuries promptly and stop participating when you need assistance.

Photos and videos

Permission to publish identifiable photos or videos is optional and separate from accepting the participation rules. Declining publication permission does not prevent an application. Notify the coordinator if you wish to change your choice.

Provisional profiles and formal admission

Provisional profiles are free. Verified players with a completed profile may pay monthly dues and view notices and sessions explicitly shared with provisional players. They do not receive access to private squad records or an Acceptance Letter. Paying dues does not guarantee later admission.

Registration, monthly dues and your Acceptance Letter

The one-time registration fee is ₦5,000. It covers processing your application, creating your player profile and completing your admission records if you are approved.

Monthly training dues are ₦3,000, including for provisional players. They support training, coaching, equipment purchases and maintenance, and routine academy operations. Dues do not pay the ₦5,000 formal registration fee and do not grant academy acceptance.

Paying the registration fee does not mean you have already been accepted. The academy must first review and approve your application. Once your application is approved and your registration payment is confirmed, your Acceptance Letter will be available in your dashboard to print or save as a PDF.

Use the receiving account instructions in Payments & receipts and report your bank transfer. The owner checks the actual bank credit before marking it PAID. Reporting a transfer alone is not proof of payment.

If the academy declines your application, your registration fee will be refunded. A pending refund notice does not mean the money has already been returned. Personal kits, boots, transport and competition charges are explained separately when applicable.

Academy membership does not promise a professional contract, scholarship or selection for a match. Coach-only accounts do not pay player registration fees or monthly training dues.
