Please read before creating your player account

We are opening provisional player accounts so players can support the academy’s growth, keep their details on record and receive academy updates while formal admission remains a separate process.

1. Create your provisional profile — free

Create a login, verify your email and complete your player profile. You do not pay ₦5,000 to create this profile. You can then view training schedules, events and notifications that the academy shares with provisional players. Your account is a pre-registration record; it does not mean you have been formally accepted.

2. Pay monthly training dues — ₦3,000 per month

Monthly dues apply to provisional players and formally accepted players. They help the academy buy needed equipment, maintain existing equipment, support coaching and training, and meet routine running costs as the academy grows.

After saving your profile, open Payments & receipts, choose monthly dues and the month or months you are paying for, then follow the bank-transfer instructions shown there. Report your transfer in the portal. The owner must check that the money has reached the academy’s account before confirming payment and making your receipt available. A submitted report is still awaiting verification; do not pay again simply because it is pending.

Example: ₦3,000 covers one calendar month of dues; ₦9,000 covers three. Dues do not include or reduce the separate ₦5,000 formal registration fee, and a dues receipt is not an Acceptance Letter.

3. Apply for formal admission — a separate ₦5,000 fee

When you want to apply for formal academy admission, follow the formal application step in your dashboard. The one-time ₦5,000 registration fee covers processing your formal application and completing your admission records if approved. Its payment must be verified before you submit the formal application for review. Paying this fee alone does not mean you have been accepted.

4. Receive an Acceptance Letter after approval

The academy must review and approve your formal application. Your Acceptance Letter becomes available in your dashboard only after that approval and confirmation of your ₦5,000 registration payment. You can then print it or save it as a PDF. Creating a profile or paying monthly dues does not unlock the letter.

For now: start with your free provisional profile, pay your monthly dues through the portal and check shared academy updates. Formal admission is a separate step.
