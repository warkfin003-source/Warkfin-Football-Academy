About us

Purpose, discipline and development.

We want the habits learned in training to make a difference on and off the field.

Who we are

Warkfin Football Academy is a developing football training community bringing together students, non-students aged 18 and above, and adults who want to improve their game.

What we do

We focus on football fundamentals, positional understanding, physical preparation and teamwork through practical sessions and small-sided games.

Our Team

Founder / Coordinator: Warkfin Emmanuel.

Our 27 listed players bring a mix of attacking, midfield, defensive and goalkeeping interests. Coaching and support appointments will be announced when confirmed.

Meet G.O.A.T. Squad A

Our Plans

Our proposed next steps are to establish consistent training, assess players fairly, strengthen defensive coverage and build a reliable coaching and support structure.

WF Academy

A structured foundation for better football.

Development starts with consistent effort and coaching that matches the group.

Our Courses

Proposed training modules—not separately confirmed paid courses. Session availability, duration and any fees will be announced by the coordinator.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Ball mastery & technique

First touch, close control, passing, dribbling and finishing.

02 / POSITION PLAY

Understand your role

Defensive shape, midfield support, attacking movement and transitions.

03 / GOALKEEPING

Protect the goal

Ready position, footwork, handling, distribution and communication with defenders.

04 / GAME HABITS

Move, communicate & decide

Football movement, scanning, teamwork and decisions in small-sided games.

Our Approach

Start from each player's current ability. Demonstrate clearly, allow repeated practice, offer useful feedback and recognise progress.

Our Training Methodology

  1. Show: explain and demonstrate the skill.
  2. Practise: repeat in manageable drills.
  3. Apply: use it in a game situation.
  4. Review: give feedback and choose the next goal.

    Respect. Responsibility. Care.

    Academy Rules & Welfare

    Clear expectations for players, coaches and volunteers. Individual attendance and assessments remain private.

    Conduct and commitment

    Arrive on time in complete football kit. Confirm availability privately and explain absences directly to the coordinator. Respect teammates, opponents and officials. Coaches must give constructive feedback without humiliation, threats, physical punishment, bullying, discrimination, sexual misconduct or harassment.

    Coaches and players should agree personal improvement goals privately. No public attendance rankings or comparisons of commitment are published.

    Under-18 consent and supervision

    Before an under-18 player participates, a parent or guardian must provide written participation consent, emergency contact details and agreed arrival and collection arrangements. Completing the online enquiry alone does not provide guardian consent.

    Management must confirm suitable supervision before the session. Adults must avoid isolated one-to-one situations with children and include parents or guardians in necessary communications. Changing arrangements must protect privacy and separate adults from children appropriately; photography in changing areas is prohibited.

    Injuries and session preparation

    Before each session, organisers must check the playing area, identify the responsible adult and first-aid support, check emergency contacts, and agree how urgent medical help and transport will be obtained. Do not assume a medic is available.

    Stop play and alert the responsible adult if someone is injured or unwell. Seek appropriate medical help, contact the emergency contact or guardian, and record the incident privately. Players must not be pressured to continue when hurt.

    Speak up about a concern

    Report bullying, harassment, abuse or unsafe behaviour privately to Warkfin Emmanuel. Share only information needed to explain the concern. Reports should be handled discreetly, but information may need to be shared with appropriate services to protect someone.

    If a concern involves the coordinator, or someone is in immediate danger, seek help from a trusted adult and the relevant emergency, police or child-protection services. You do not need academy permission to seek help.

    Request a private welfare conversation
    Photos, information and assessments

    Obtain permission before publishing identifiable photos or videos, including parent or guardian permission for under-18s and the young person's agreement. Participation consent does not automatically include media consent.

    Attendance, emergency contacts and coach assessments must be held privately and shared only with authorised people who need them. This website has no private assessment portal. Contact the coordinator about corrections, media removal or information already shared.

    Fees and academy status

    No fee schedule or refund terms have been announced. Before any payment is requested, players or guardians should receive written information about the amount, what it covers, cancellation and refund terms, and the authorised payment method.

    No formal registration, football affiliation or competition approval is claimed on this website. Management must confirm the requirements that apply before offering paid programmes or entering formal competitions.

    Safeguarding resource: FIFA Guardians guidance. This reference does not imply FIFA affiliation or endorsement.
